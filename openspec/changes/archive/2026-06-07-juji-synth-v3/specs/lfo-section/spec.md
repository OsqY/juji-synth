## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Dual LFO generators
The synthesizer SHALL provide two LFOs displayed as a full-width tab panel with large 3D knobs for rate and depth, and waveform visualization.

#### Scenario: LFO free-running
- **WHEN** LFO is enabled and key is not held
- **THEN** LFO cycles continuously at its configured rate

#### Scenario: LFO panel full-width
- **WHEN** the LFO tab is selected
- **THEN** the LFO panel fills the available width with large rate and depth knobs, waveform selectors, and a waveform shape visualization
