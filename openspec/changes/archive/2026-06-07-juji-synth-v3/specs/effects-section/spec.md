## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Effects processing system
The synthesizer SHALL include effects displayed as a full-width tab panel with large 3D knobs for each effect parameter and LED-style bypass toggles.

#### Scenario: Effects enabled
- **WHEN** effects are enabled
- **THEN** dry signal is routed through effect processors before output

#### Scenario: Effects panel full-width
- **WHEN** the FX tab is selected
- **THEN** the effects panel fills the available width with large knobs grouped by effect type (Reverb, Delay, Distortion) with LED bypass toggles
